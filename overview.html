Fast, private, and secure writing — by design.

Munetios OmniWrite is a modern, open-source writing platform that encrypts your documents directly in your browser. Your content stays private, offline-ready, and fully under your control.

Features

  • End-to-end encrypted documents
  • Folders
  • Lock Documents
  • Offline-first design
  • Images and media support
  • Liquid Glass interface
  • Dark mode and appearance themes
  • No account required
  • Fully open source

Security & Privacy

All documents in Munetios OmniWrite are encrypted locally using modern cryptography. Encryption keys are generated on your device, stored securely, and never transmitted to Munetios servers.

This zero-knowledge design ensures that only you can read your content. If encryption keys are lost or reset, documents cannot be recovered — a core property of true end-to-end encryption.

FAQ

  • Is OmniWrite open source? Yes. OmniWrite is open source and transparent by design.
  • Can Munetios read my documents? No. Encryption keys never leave your device.
  • Do I need an account? No account or sign-in is required.
  • Does OmniWrite work offline? Yes, offline support is built in.
  • Can I use AI features in OmniWrite? As of now, AI features are not integrated into OmniWrite.
